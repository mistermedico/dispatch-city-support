Dispatch City Support

Dispatch City is a fictional offline strategy game. It is not an emergency service and must not be used during a real emergency.

Common questions

How is progress saved?

Progress is saved locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove local progress.

Does the game require an account or internet connection?

No. The current version requires no account and its core gameplay works offline.

The app is not responding

Close and reopen the app. If the issue continues, restart the device and make sure iOS is up to date.

Accessibility

Accessibility options are available from Settings in the app.
