Dispatch City Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026.

Dispatch City is designed as an offline game. The current version does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer. Deleting the app may delete this local data.

Permissions and third parties

The current version does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or tracking permission. It does not contain enabled advertising, analytics, or third-party login services.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes

If a future version adds online features or data collection, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
